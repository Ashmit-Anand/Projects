# Magic Calculator 🪄

A fun calculator-based magic trick built using MIT App Inventor.

The calculator looks and behaves like a normal calculator, but contains
a hidden interaction that allows the performer to make it reveal a
number entered earlier in the trick.

## How It Works

Long press **7** in the app and enter the number you want the calculator
to reveal at the end.

To perform the trick:

1. Ask the person for a random **5-digit number**.
2. Ask for the **last two digits of their mobile number**.
3. Multiply the two numbers.
4. Divide the result by the **5th digit of their mobile number**.
5. Press **=**.

The calculator will display the number that was secretly entered by
long pressing **7**.

## Files

- `MagicCalculator.aia` — MIT App Inventor source project

## Demo

The trick is designed to be performed with another person and works
